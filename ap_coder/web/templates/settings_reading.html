AI reviewer

The chat model in LM Studio checks every invoice against its pages, as an AP clerk would before posting it: the header, each line and its GL account, the taxes and the math. What it reads differently is marked Check; when it is not sure, a person looks. With it on, no invoice is approved without a person until it agrees.

For IT: how long it may take

It thinks first, and is asked again about anything it disputed until it is sure. It runs in the background, between OvisOCR2's invoices.
