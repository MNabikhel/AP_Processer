AP Coder makes a temporary password and shows it once; they choose their own at their first login.

The roles

  • Read-only: looks at the review queue, invoices, reports, exports, Insights, Spend, Activity and Help. Changes nothing.
  • Clerk: also imports invoices, reviews, checks and approves them (the first approval), parks, rejects and reopens them, approves in bulk and makes export batches.
  • Approver: also gives the second approval to invoices over the approval limit. It must be another person than the first approver.
  • Admin: everything, including Settings, users, the GL accounts and tax setup, imports, JD Edwards, backups and restore, and deleting.
