Where invoices are delivered to you (sales tax)

Sales tax follows where the goods are delivered to you (services: generally where you are), not where the vendor is or ships from. A vendor in Ontario shipping from BC to your site in BC charges GST and BC PST. When an invoice prints no ship-to, AP Coder uses the province of the cost center its lines are coded to, else your province. A ship-to printed on the invoice always wins.
