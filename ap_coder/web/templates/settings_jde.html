Province → tax area and explanation code

The tax area names are placeholders: use your E1 tax areas (F4008). Codes: V = GST/HST/QST recoverable, C = GST + seller-charged PST (PST in the distribution), B = self-assessed PST, E = exempt. Invoices with no tax charged use the exempt code; PST provinces without PST charged use the self-assessed code.
