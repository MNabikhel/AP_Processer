Sends the company's finance hub on Microsoft Fabric six tables: invoices, their lines, approvals, the Activity log, accuracy (what AP Coder proposed against what was approved) and runs. Only what changed since the last run goes. The invoices' PDFs never leave AP Coder: each invoice carries a link back to its page here. The tables and their columns are described in docs/FINANCE_HUB.md (version {{ hub_version }}).
