# Coding policy notes (one rule per line; lines starting with # are ignored)
- Capitalisation threshold is 2,500.00 CAD per unit; IT hardware below this goes to 6010, at or above goes to 1500.
- Software or support contracts covering more than 3 months that are paid upfront go to 1550 Prepaid Expenses.
- Freight on inventory/raw material purchases goes to 5100 with the same cost center as the goods; otherwise use 6800.
- Cloud consumption for production workloads is coded to CC410; internal/dev tooling to CC400.
- Training and conference fees are coded to the attendee's department; default to CC300 if no attendee or department is stated.
- Legal fees always go to CC800 regardless of the requesting department.
