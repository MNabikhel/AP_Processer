- **D-160** · **The review screen made light: the vendor list fetched and kept, page pictures drawn once for all workers, a reader poll of a few bytes, each vendor's invoices kept** (scale goal, phase 2 part 2).
  - **Why:** on the 100k database the 20,000-vendor list was rebuilt for every invoice opened (about 400 ms of a 500 ms page) and was 3.3 MB of its 3.6 MB. Each web worker drew the page pictures it was asked for (40 ms, 115 ms at scale 2) into its own 96 MB. The reader poll put its panel in again every 20 seconds. Every check read all of its vendor's invoices (220 ms for a vendor of 7,800).
  - **The vendor list:** `GET /review/vendors.json?v=<version>` (READ, as the screen; no bank data in it). `invoice.vendor_list` keeps, per process and per vendor-list stamp (`summary_memo`, `changes/vendors`; without a vendor master also the `approved_vendors` topic): the JSON, it gzipped, its version (its hash), its count, and the first vendor of each vendor key and GST/HST business number. The page holds `#vendor-seed` (the address, enforced, the count, and the vendors it speaks of as it opens), so what the screen says of the vendor is right at once. An editable screen fetches the list as it opens. The fill from the list waits for it. An open vendor list is drawn again when it comes (`picker.refresh`).
  - **Browser caching:** on a laptop, `private, max-age=31536000, immutable` while `v` is the list's version (`no-cache` for another), with a weak ETag (304).
    - **On the server B-1070 stands (decided):** every answer behind the login is `no-store`, the vendor list and the page pictures included, so nothing of the company's stays in a shared computer's browser cache after logout. Each editable screen fetches the list from the worker's memo: 370 KB gzipped and about 7 ms of CPU, on the company network. Exempting them is one line in `identity._no_store`, a security decision, not a speed one.
  - **Page pictures (server):** in `<data>/.cache/pages/<2>/`, named by the file's SHA-256, the page, the scale, the DPI, the size cap and `DRAWING`. Written under a name of its own, then `os.replace`; on Windows retried, and left when the same picture is there (B-1689's way). At most `PAGES_KEPT_BYTES` (1 GB), the oldest written removed first, down to three quarters, never the newest. A full disk draws each time. A worker keeps no picture in memory there. The laptop is unchanged.
  - **The reader poll:** `?seen=<tag>` (`reader_tag`; the page's `data-tag`). While the panel is what the page shows, the answer is `same`, without it, and nothing is drawn again.
  - **Each vendor's invoices:** `Store.vendor_invoices` kept per process for the vendors asked most recently (`VENDOR_ROWS_KEPT`, 20,000 rows, ~14 MB). Stamped by `summary_id` and `changes/vendor/<key>`, counted by three triggers on invoices (insert, delete, an update of what it lists; an invoice moved to another vendor counts for both). A check's confidence write is not listed. Read inside a write under way: afresh, not kept. The rows no longer carry `adjusted_confidence`/`requires_review` (read by nothing).
  - **Measured (100k, four vendors in turn):** page 544 → 147 ms and 3.61 MB → 300 KB; check 93 → 66 ms; a picture read back 8 ms against 40-115 ms drawn; a poll 210 → 66 bytes.
  - **Not done:** `reading_status` costs 9 ms on every page, queue and poll (5.5 ms of it `supplier_templates_count`); a JD Edwards sync rewrites every vendor's `erp_bank`/`erp_hold`, which resets the vendor list in every worker (phase 4); the pilot tester's page pictures (`pages/manager.py`) could use the shared cache.
